# Causal Validity and Safety of Offline RL

## Core question

Historical dose trajectories are generated by clinicians and care systems, not by randomized policy assignment. A learned policy must therefore state which policy effect it claims to estimate and why the logged data support that claim.

## Required design record

- Target population, decision times, actions, outcomes, horizon, and target policy.
- Observed state variables and plausible unobserved confounders.
- Behavior-policy/action support analysis: identify actions that lack comparable historical support.
- Handling of censoring, missingness, changing measurement practice, and site/time shift.
- OPE estimator(s), uncertainty intervals, sensitivity analyses, and policy constraints.
- Escalation trigger: simulator qualification, external replay, or prospective study before any clinical use.

## Interpretation guardrails

- OPE estimates are conditional on design and identification assumptions; they do not establish causal safety alone.
- A policy should not recommend actions outside documented clinical constraints or poorly supported regions of the logged data.
- Report patient- and subgroup-level policy inspection alongside aggregate value estimates.
