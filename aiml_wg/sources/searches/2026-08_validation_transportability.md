# Focused Search — Validation, Transportability, and Decision Usefulness

**Date:** 2026-08-29  
**Purpose:** Support V-01/V-02, benchmark pilot criteria, and white paper Section D.  
**Databases:** PubMed and primary journal/regulatory pages where needed.

## Search concepts

```text
(pharmacokinetic OR pharmacometric OR PK/PD OR clinical machine learning)
AND (external validation OR temporal validation OR calibration OR transportability
     OR "dataset shift" OR applicability domain OR decision utility)
```

## Inclusion criteria

- Directly informs external/temporal validation, calibration, transportability, or decision-level evaluation.
- Methodological source or drug-development/clinical ML application with transferable evaluation lessons.
- Verifiable primary metadata and an identifiable destination in the WG plans.

## Included records

- `cabitza_2021_external_validation` — distinguishes discrimination, utility, and calibration in external validation.
- `adamson_2026_transportability` — drug-development-relevant transportability principles, including transparent assumptions and uncertainty communication.

## Processing

Records were captured at `abstract_only` depth, indexed, and linked to the validation-pilot and white-paper plans. Full-text review is deferred until a pilot dataset/COU is selected.
