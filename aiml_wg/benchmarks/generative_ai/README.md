# Generative AI Pharmacometric Comparison

**Status:** Protocol draft v0.1; Phase 1 is pilot-ready but not preregistered.
**Systems in scope:** Claude, Codex, and Antigravity, recorded as exact product configurations rather than brand-level abstractions.
**Activities:** M4.5-A1 through M4.5-A11.

## Files

- `protocol_v0.1.md` — common experimental design, activity matrix, analysis, gates, and stopping rules.
- `phase1_prompt_set_v0.1.md` — frozen candidate prompts and adjudication keys for M4.5-A2 and M4.5-A6.
- `evaluation_card_v0.1.md` — blinded scoring form and activity-specific modules.
- `run_manifest.schema.json` — required machine-readable metadata for each independent run.
- `pilot_readiness.md` — completed checks and external decisions required before data collection.
- `project_memory.md` — durable decisions and considerations to carry into future sessions.
