## The reframe

The polished agenda is written for a staffed WG working through six group decisions. With **3 attendees (SK, ZK, MC)** the meeting is really: *align the three founders, stress-test against MC's expertise, and leave with an 8-week plan and a recruiting commitment.* Don't run it as a 90-minute six-decision workshop — that will feel hollow with three people. Compress to ~45–60 min and spend the time on the two live decisions plus ownership.

## Replace the "Ownership Signals" segment

The agenda's 8-row ownership-signals table assumes a room of volunteers to absorb workstreams. With 3 people, don't ask "who wants to lead Pillar 4" — there's no one to raise a hand, and it underscores the empty seats. Replace that segment with a **3-person work split for the next 8 weeks** (from `phase1_scope.md §3`):

| Piece | Suggested owner | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scope & Call for Participation brief (2–4 pp) | SK | Recruiting magnet; becomes front of scoping paper |
| Technical seed note (conformal-prediction OR neural-ODE identifiability) | ZK or MC | Pick ONE based on §Decision-1/4 outcome |
| Journal club launch (month 2) | MC | Doubles as recruiting funnel |
| Recruiting outreach | SK + ZK | Each personally invites ~5 named people this week |

## Don't skip: the recruiting commitment

This is the real Phase 1 deliverable and the agenda buries it. Before close, get concrete:
- **Target:** e.g., 6 active members by end of Month 2.
- **Channels:** MCS SIG list → sister WGs (Optimal Control, SAUQ) → AI/ML SIG cross-post → ACoP 2026 in-person. (See `phase1_scope.md §4`.)
- **Owner:** name one. Recruiting fails when it's everyone's job.
